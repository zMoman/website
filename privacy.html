Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 19, 2026

17912561 CANADA INC. ("we," "our," or "the Company") is committed to maintaining straightforward, transparent data practices. This Privacy Policy outlines how information is handled across our mobile applications and digital products.

1. Data Minimization & Privacy-by-Design

We strictly enforce a data minimization framework. Our core development philosophy dictates that user data belongs on the user's device. Our applications are engineered to process and store your information locally whenever possible.

2. Information Collection

We do not operate backend registration servers, maintain user profile databases, harvest behavioral telemetry data, or track your activity across third-party applications. If an application requires a configuration file or local preference state, that dataset remains contained within the sandboxed directory structure provided by the operating system.

3. Third-Party Frameworks

Certain applications published by the Company may utilize core native platform frameworks provided by Apple Inc. (such as iCloud for optional cross-device synchronization). Any data routed through these native ecosystems is encrypted and governed directly under Apple's standard developer provisions and customer privacy frameworks.

4. Contact & Regulatory Inquiries

For questions regarding this policy or our technical architectures, please contact our administrative desk directly at admin@17912561.ca.
